📖 Compound Inequalities: Comprehensive Theory & Video Hub

A compound inequality combines two simple inequalities into a single statement using the words "AND" or "OR". Review the descriptive notes and master the concepts with our curated Khan Academy video lessons below.

1. "AND" Compound Inequalities (Intersection)

\[a < x < b \iff (x > a) \text{ AND } (x < b)\]

Both conditions must be satisfied simultaneously. Graphically, it represents the overlap (intersection) between two boundary points.

2. "OR" Compound Inequalities (Union)

\[x < a \text{ OR } x > b\]

At least one of the conditions must be satisfied. Graphically, it represents the combined range (union) pointing outward in opposite directions.
